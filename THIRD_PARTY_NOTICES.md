- License legal text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en
- Retrieved 24 October 2026.

The publisher states these maps are based on NASA elevation and imagery data, with colors tuned using spacecraft photographs. Some unmapped terrain is filled and colors are adjusted by the original publisher. The ten included JPEG images are losslessly base64 encoded in local JavaScript chunk modules (`assets/tex-{kind}-{n}.js`) and reconstructed as data URLs by `assets/textures.js`. This storage conversion does not modify the underlying image bytes. The original JPEG files are not included in this text-only edition. Moon and Saturn ring image files are omitted; the app renders Saturn’s rings procedurally. Display rendering, lighting, color management, and cropping may alter their appearance.
